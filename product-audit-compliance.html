Business Problems Solved

What this platform actually fixes.

PROBLEM · 01

Paper-Based Audits Don't Scale

Multi-site operations drown in paper checklists. Findings take weeks to surface. Trends are invisible.

PROBLEM · 02

Corrective Actions Leak

CAPA tickets get raised, then forgotten. No closed-loop verification. Half of findings never get fixed.

PROBLEM · 03

Compliance is a Last-Minute Scramble

External audits trigger month-long fire drills to reconstruct trails from scattered records.

PROBLEM · 04

Inconsistent Across Sites

Each location has its own checklist, scoring rubric, reporting cadence. Apples-and-oranges roll-ups.

PROBLEM · 05

Risk Register Lives in Excel

Top 10 risks reviewed quarterly in a slide deck. No daily signal. New risks surface from incidents, not foresight.

PROBLEM · 06

Auditor Productivity Plateaus

Inspectors re-key the same data into 3 systems. Hours of every visit lost to admin.
