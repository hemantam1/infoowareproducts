Six Agent Patterns

All our AI reduces to six reusable patterns.

Each agent in each product is an instance of one of these six. That unification is what lets us ship faster — and what makes AI quality compound across our customer base.

AGENT · 01

Knowledge Agents

RAG-powered assistants that turn enterprise documents, SOPs, contracts, regulations into instant, cited answers — grounded in your data, not the open web.

  • Vector + keyword hybrid retrieval
  • Citation back to source
  • Role-based access control
AGENT · 02

Workflow Agents

Automation that drives processes end-to-end across modules — onboarding flows, approval chains, dispatch logic. No more glue code or ETL piping.

  • Cross-module orchestration
  • Human-in-the-loop checkpoints
  • Audit trail of every decision
AGENT · 03

Sales Agents

Lead qualification, deal coaching, next-best-action — embedded directly in CRM, field sales and customer engagement modules.

  • Outlet-level next-best-action
  • Win-loss pattern detection
  • Live coaching on rep mobile
AGENT · 04

Compliance Agents

Audit-ready, alert-on-violation copilots that scan workflows continuously and catch gaps before regulators or auditors do.

  • Continuous policy scanning
  • Pre-emptive risk flags
  • Auto-generated audit packages
AGENT · 05

Learning Agents

Personalized study paths, auto-evaluation and skill-gap detection across institutions, corporate L&D programs, and customer enablement.

  • Adaptive learning paths
  • Auto-grading of open answers
  • Skill-gap heat maps
AGENT · 06

Customer Support Agents

First-line WhatsApp / chat / voice support that resolves common cases end-to-end — and escalates intelligently with full context.

  • WhatsApp + Voice + Web
  • CRM-aware responses
  • Smart escalation to humans

How We Build It

Production AI. Not demoware.

PRINCIPLE

Grounded in your data

Every agent is grounded in your tenant's data — never trained on, never leaked across customers. Vector stores are isolated per tenant.

PRINCIPLE

Eval & feedback by default

Every agent ships with an evaluation harness and a feedback loop. We measure quality continuously and improve from real usage.

PRINCIPLE

Human-in-the-loop where it matters

High-risk actions — prescriptions, large transactions, compliance decisions — always route through a human checkpoint with full context.

PRINCIPLE

Model-agnostic by design

OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source — we pick the right model per workflow and swap as the frontier shifts. You're not locked in.
